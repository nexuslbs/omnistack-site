Company overview

  • Duolingo, Inc. is an American educational technology company that produces learning apps and provides language certification. — Duolingo - Wikipedia (accessed 2026-09-27)
  • Duolingo was founded in 2011 by Luis von Ahn and Severin Hacker, and its Class A shares trade on Nasdaq under the ticker DUOL. — Duolingo - Wikipedia (accessed 2026-09-27)
  • Duolingo's catalogue spans 42 languages plus courses in music, math and chess. — Duolingo - Wikipedia (accessed 2026-09-27)

Business model and offer

  • Duolingo runs a freemium model: the core learning experience is free with ads, while subscriptions remove ads and add unlimited energy and generative-AI features. — Duolingo - Wikipedia (accessed 2026-09-27)
  • Subscription is the largest revenue line: $873.44 million of the $1,038 million reported for fiscal year 2025, against $79.73 million from advertising and $42.01 million from the Duolingo English Test. — Duolingo (DUOL) Financials - StockAnalysis (accessed 2026-09-27)
  • Duolingo also earns from in-app purchases of virtual currency and power-ups and from the paid Duolingo English Test certification. — Duolingo - Wikipedia (accessed 2026-09-27)
