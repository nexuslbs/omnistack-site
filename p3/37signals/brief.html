Company overview

  • 37signals is an American web software company based in Chicago, Illinois, founded in 1999 by Jason Fried, Carlos Segura and Ernest Kim. — 37signals - Wikipedia (accessed 2026-09-27)
  • It is privately held, and Wikipedia lists 34 employees as of 2021. — 37signals - Wikipedia (accessed 2026-09-27)
  • Basecamp, the company's flagship project management product, was built out of 37signals' own need to manage client projects when it was still a design firm. — Basecamp - Where we came from (accessed 2026-09-27)
  • 37signals describes itself as having 27 profitable years in business, zero debt and private ownership. — Basecamp - Pricing (accessed 2026-09-27)

Business model and offer

  • 37signals sells web applications under several brands, including Basecamp, HEY, Fizzy and ONCE. — 37signals (accessed 2026-09-27)
  • Basecamp charges fixed prices with no per-user fees, and Studio and higher plans include unlimited users. — Basecamp - Pricing (accessed 2026-09-27)
  • The ONCE line is sold for one fixed price and is self-hosted by the customer; its first two products are Campfire for group chat and Writebook for publishing a book online. — ONCE - Introducing ONCE (accessed 2026-09-27)
  • Ruby on Rails, the open-source web framework created at 37signals, is maintained by a community of over six thousand contributors. — Ruby on Rails: Accelerate your agents with convention over configuration (accessed 2026-09-27)

Pricing signals

  • Basecamp's plan ladder is Free (1 project, 1 GB storage, 5 users), Freelancer at $25 per month, Studio at $59 per month, Pro at $99 per month and Unlimited at $299 per month billed annually. — Basecamp - Pricing (accessed 2026-09-27)
  • Basecamp says 2,026 organizations signed up in the last week and offers 10 percent off for registered 501(c)(3) non-profits and free use for K-12 and university class work. — Basecamp - Pricing (accessed 2026-09-27)
  • HEY offers a 30-day free trial with no credit card required. — HEY - A delightfully fresh take on email + calendar, from 37signals (accessed 2026-09-27)
  • ONCE is sold for one fixed price rather than a recurring subscription. — ONCE - Introducing ONCE (accessed 2026-09-27)
